## Your Top 3 Stories for This Interview

### Story 1: Ankr 15x Revenue (THE STORY)

**Use for:** Why Alchemy, customer discovery, B2B strategy, finding PMF

**Quick pitch:**
> "At Ankr, I was competing directly against Alchemy in the RPC infrastructure space. We were losing the developer segment race—couldn't out-Alchemy Alchemy with 5 engineers. I pivoted our primary customer from developers to protocols. Through 15+ protocol interviews, I discovered protocols were underserved and had 10x the LTV of developer accounts. We built Advanced APIs as differentiation, redesigned pricing with SLA guarantees, and ran outbound sales to protocol BD teams. Result: 15x revenue growth in 8 months, from $130K to $2M ARR."

**Key details to remember:**
- Competitors: Infura, Alchemy (the company you're interviewing with!)
- Insight: Protocols had 10x LTV of developer accounts
- What didn't work: Priced protocol tier too low initially, SDK was over-engineered, marketing content didn't convert (outreach was 10x better)

### Story 2: L2 Protocol Integrations at Anchorage

**Use for:** Scale, process, cross-functional leadership, working with protocols

**Quick pitch:**
> "At Anchorage, rapid L2 growth meant constant integration requests with no standardized process—12+ weeks per chain. I built a RICE-based evaluation framework with parallel workstreams. Security, compliance, and engineering ran simultaneously instead of sequentially. Created a RACI matrix, negotiated SLAs (2-week assessment, 6-week full integration), and trained BD to self-serve initial scoring. Result: 40% faster cycle time, first time shipping 3 protocols simultaneously (Base, Arbitrum, Plume), saved $200K in hiring costs."

**Key protocols:** Optimism, Base, Arbitrum, Plume, Citrea, BOB, Aztec (many are likely Alchemy customers)

### Story 3: ETH Staking Zero Slashing (Galaxy Win)

**Use for:** Technical depth, stakeholder management, compliance, institutional clients

**Quick pitch:**
> "Galaxy and ETF providers wanted staking yield but existing solutions lacked institutional custody controls. Leadership pushed for a 3-week launch using third-party staking providers. I presented a compliance risk matrix showing third-party key management was an audit blocker. Chose in-house validators with multi-client architecture—Lighthouse 40%, Prysm 30%, Teku 20%, Nimbus 10%. Built redundant slashing DB that misses attestations rather than risk slashing. Result: Zero slashing events since launch. Galaxy specifically cited 'full key ownership' as why they chose us over competitors."

**Key insight:** "In regulated crypto infrastructure, the moat isn't technical innovation—it's trust, auditability, and the paper trail."
